# Finance-Related Journals Across Medicine, Neuroscience, Psychology, and Sociology
**English Markdown working guide for PubMed searching**

> **Scope note**
> This is a **broad, high-yield, cross-disciplinary working list**, not a mathematically exhaustive census of every journal that may ever publish finance-related work. The goal is to help you search topics such as **financial stress, financial well-being, personal finance, health costs, medical financial hardship, consumer decision-making, risk, reward, debt, saving, investment behavior, retirement, inequality, and household economics** through a biomedical and behavioral lens.
>
> Because **PubMed is biomedical-first**, some excellent consumer, finance, or sociology journals may have **limited or inconsistent PubMed coverage**. When a journal-specific PubMed query returns sparse results, supplement with **Google Scholar, Scopus, Web of Science, PsycINFO, or EconLit**.

## How to Use This File
- Start with the **journal-specific PubMed template**.
- Replace or expand keywords depending on your subtopic.
- Use `[Title/Abstract]` for flexible keyword capture.
- Use `[Journal]` to constrain to one journal.
- Add date filters, age filters, or methodology filters when needed.

## Suggested Next-Step Workflow
1. Run the **broad topic template** first.
2. Identify recurrent concepts and terminology.
3. Narrow using one or two **journal filters**.
4. Add:
   - publication year filters
   - study type filters
   - age group filters
   - country filters
5. Export your best search strings into a reusable note.

## Optional Filters You Can Append
- **Review articles**:
```text
AND (review[Publication Type] OR systematic[sb])
```
- **Recent 10 years**:
```text
AND ("2016/01/01"[Date - Publication] : "3000"[Date - Publication])
```
- **Adults only**:
```text
AND adult[MeSH Terms]
```
- **Humans only**:
```text
AND humans[MeSH Terms]
```
