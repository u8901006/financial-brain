Technical efficiency of public health facilities in Punjab, Pakistan: application of a two-stage data envelopment analysis.

BMC health services research · 2026-10-14

分類：Fallback

中文摘要

This study analyses the technical efficiency of public health facilities in Punjab, Pakistan, using a two-stage data envelopment analysis (DEA) approach. The study focuses on family planning service delivery, addressing suboptimal contraceptive prevalence, and service utilization. Data were collected from 100 public health facilities managed by the Primary and Secondary Health Department, with 50 from the Delivering Accelerated Family Planning in Pakistan (DAFPAK) project and 50 non-cohort facilities. DEA was used to assess technical efficiency, considering inputs such as contraceptive stock, staff availability, and facility size. In addition, we used the Simar-Wilson regression to analyse the influence of environmental factors. The average efficiency score was 78%, with variations across facility types. Basic Health Units and Rural Health Centers showed significant resource optimization gaps. A negative association was observed between the number of other healthcare providers in the catchment area and facility efficiency, indicating the importance of aligning service provision with local demand. DEA effectively identifies inefficiencies and informs resource optimization in public health facilities in Punjab. The findings offer valuable insights for improving family planning services and healthcare delivery in resource-constrained settings. Not applicable.

Lesions in the cerebellum impact cross-modal temporal predictions.

Biological psychology · 2026-10-14

分類：Fallback

中文摘要

The cerebellum (CE) supports the encoding of the precise sensory event timing and the generation of temporal predictions. Here we investigated whether focal CE lesions impact temporal predictions in a cross-modal context. Individuals with CE lesion (n=9) and healthy-matched controls (HC) were presented with visuo-auditory stimulus pairs, presented in a temporally regular (predictable) or irregular (unpredictable) manner while EEG was recorded. We hypothesized cross-modal temporal predictions to be mediated by pre-stimulus cerebello-cortical beta-band (12-25Hz) activity. In turn, we expected HC, but not CE patients, to show a modulation of pre-stimulus beta power as a function of temporal prediction. HC showed greater pre-stimulus beta-band suppression presumably related to anticipation of sound onsets, and stronger post-stimulus delta-band (1-4Hz) power in the predictable than unpredictable condition. Furthermore, they displayed a significant modulation of pre-stimulus delta-beta cross-frequency coupling as a function of temporal predictability. These effects were not observed in the CE group, suggesting a deficit in temporal predictions. These results extend the role of cerebellar predictive timing from sensorimotor to motor-independent cross-modal temporal predictions.
